# UP-001 - Fleet operator

Status: Design-derived actor hypothesis. Not validated by interview.

Source: [Design evidence](../docs/design/README.md), particularly the restricted
fleet-operator sign-in copy at lines 2266-2267, the CS2 tracking subtitle at
2305, and the small trusted group copy at 3640.

The operator tracks competitive cooldown evidence for known Steam accounts.
The represented tasks are recording observations, reviewing current
restrictions and history, anticipating later ladder changes, and maintaining
offence definitions. These are inferred tasks, not reported working practices.

The likely benefit is fewer manual calculations when comparing accounts and
checking escalation history. No frequency, financial benefit, skill level,
demographic, employer, geographic location or accessibility need is claimed.
Keyboard, assistive-technology and small-screen use are proposed inclusion
scenarios, not facts about a specific person.

Unknowns include who provides the tracked accounts, who grants access, whether
every operator can alter shared rules, how many fleets exist, whether records
belong to one person or a group, and how operators correct mistakes. See the
[decision register](../3-requirements/open-questions.md).

No administrator persona or distinct viewer/editor role is invented. A future
decision-maker may introduce those roles if access requirements support them.

Stories: [All design-derived outcomes](../2-user-stories/README.md).
